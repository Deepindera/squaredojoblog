Welcome to the SquareDojo Blog.

SquareDojo is an independent software studio. Here we will share the practical side of building products: development experiences, product updates, technical challenges, lessons learned, and the occasional personal commentary from the work along the way.

We will write about the ideas and decisions behind **Columnly**, **TextArcher**, **LogMyPoop**, and **Byostream** as they evolve. Whether a post is a small implementation note, a hard-won lesson, or a broader reflection on independent software, we are glad to have you here.
